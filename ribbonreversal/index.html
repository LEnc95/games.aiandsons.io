Sort five bundles: shortest ribbon at the top, longest at the bottom. Select 2 or more ribbons from the top, then reverse their order. Tap a ribbon to select the cut, or use ↑/↓, then Space to flip. You have 80 flips for the whole studio. No timer pressure. P pauses, R restarts, T changes colors, F toggles fullscreen.
