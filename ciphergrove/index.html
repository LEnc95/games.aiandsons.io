LETTERS FROM THE WOODLAND

Cipher Grove

Decode five hidden nature phrases. Each cipher letter always stands for the same plain letter; two cipher letters cannot share a plain letter. Gold leaves are given. Use the word clue or reveal a letter when stuck.

Tap a tile, then a letter below. Repeated symbols change together.
←/→ select cipher · A–Z assign plain letter · Backspace erase · Enter check/continue
Escape or Pause freezes play · Shift+U undo · Shift+H reveal · Shift+R restart
Shift+F fullscreen · Escape exits fullscreen. Three incorrect checks end the walk.
No time limit. Reveals are optional and do not cost an inspection.
