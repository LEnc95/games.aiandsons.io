Goal: land on each glowing branch and collect every acorn before reaching the nest. Tune the dotted arc with Up/Down for angle and Left/Right for power, then press Space. You can also drag a path from the squirrel and release. Three missed branches end the climb. P pauses, R restarts, T changes the canopy palette, and F toggles fullscreen.
