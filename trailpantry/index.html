THE LITTLE OUTFITTER · LOGIC COLLECTION

Trail Pantry

A small backpack. A precise picnic. Choose supply bundles to match both food and water for five woodland hikes.

Match the food AND water targets exactly, using no more than the listed slots. Each bundle uses one slot.
Tap a bundle to add/remove it. Arrows move the cursor · Space toggles · Enter seals or continues.
Wrong seals cost one trail pass. Three wrong seals end the trip. Editing is free; there is no time limit.
C empties the pack · P pauses · R restarts · F fullscreen · Escape exits.
