THE COURTYARD COLORIST

Festival Flags

Dye every flag with A, B, or C. Flags joined by a string must have different letters. Decorate five festival courtyards with just three dyes.

Tap a flag to cycle blank → A → B → C. Or select its numbered button and choose a dye.
Arrows select a flag · 0–3 dye · Space cycles · Enter starts or continues · V inspects
U undo · C clear · P pause · R restart · F fullscreen · Escape exits.
Three failed inspections end the festival. Undo and clear are free; no time limit.
