A LITTLE PAPER. A CLEVER ORDER.

Leafpress

Bind every paper bundle into one book. Select two bundles, then merge: the press spends ink equal to their combined weight. New bundles can merge again, so order matters! Finish five books within their ink budgets before three failed inspections.

1–6 toggle a bundle · ← / → move cursor · Space toggle cursor
M merge selected pair · Enter inspect / continue · U undo · C reset book
P or Escape pause · R restart all · F fullscreen · Escape exits fullscreen.
Undo and reset restore ink. Merge count keeps all your work. No time limit.
Tip: small bundles get reprinted often. Try merging the two lightest first.
