Goal: watch which shell hides the pearl, follow every swap, then choose it. Find four pearls on each of five shores. Three mistakes end the voyage. Use arrows or A/D and Space, tap a shell, or use the buttons. P pauses, R restarts, T changes tide colors, and F toggles fullscreen.
