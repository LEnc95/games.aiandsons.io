THE POCKET MUSEUM · CORRIDOR LOGIC

Gallery Watch

A tiny museum, a handful of docents. Choose rooms so every corridor has a watcher at either end. Cover five gallery maps within their staff budgets.

Tap a room or its numbered button to station or remove a docent. A solid corridor is watched; a dashed corridor needs a docent at one of its two numbered endpoints. Line crossings are not rooms.
Use at most the staff budget, then Inspect. Three incomplete inspections end the tour. Explore, undo, and clear for free; there is no time limit.
1–8 toggle rooms · Arrows move cursor · Space toggles · Enter starts or continues
V inspect · U undo · C clear map · P pause · R restart · F fullscreen · Escape exits.
