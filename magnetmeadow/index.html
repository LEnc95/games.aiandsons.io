Goal: fill every outlined pair with a magnet or grass. The edge clues show how many + and − poles belong in each row and column; equal poles may not touch side-to-side. Desktop: arrows move, Space cycles, Z places +−, X places −+, and C plants grass. Touch: tap a pair to cycle. I inspects, H hints, U undoes, P pauses, R restarts, T changes the field, and F toggles fullscreen.
