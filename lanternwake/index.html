Goal: collect every lantern before the ferry reaches the far dock. Steer with Up/Down or W/S, hold the on-screen buttons, or press and drag above or below the ferry. Currents continuously bend your wake. Avoid driftwood: three collisions sink the ferry. P pauses, R restarts, T changes the wake palette, and F toggles fullscreen.
