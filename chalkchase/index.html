Reach the flag after collecting stars 1–4 in order. Follow the open squares without stepping on your own chalk line. A crossed line erases the current board; three erases end the run. Use arrows, WASD, the buttons, or swipe. P pauses, R restarts, T changes colors, and F toggles fullscreen.
