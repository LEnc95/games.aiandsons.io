Oven Order

A LITTLE PLANNING. A WARM WELCOME.

One oven, one bake at a time. All tickets are ready at minute zero.
Select a ticket, move it earlier or later, then bake the whole queue.
Each ticket shows bake minutes and its latest finish. Finishing exactly on time counts!
Keys 1–6 select · ← / → move · Enter bake / continue · P pause · R restart · F fullscreen.
A late batch costs one apron, even if several orders are late. Three end the day.
Reset queue is free. No planning timer. One oven minute takes half a second.
