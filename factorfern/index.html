A LITTLE GARDEN OF NUMBERS

Factor Fern

Grow five factor ferns. Select a numbered pod, then choose a divisor to split it into two whole-number factors greater than one. Finish when every pod is prime: divisible only by itself and one. Gold pods are finished.

Tap a pod then a divisor. All divisors you need are available below.
←/→ select pod · 2–9 split by that number · U undo · Enter start/continue
P or Escape pause · R restart · F fullscreen · Escape exits fullscreen.
Three invalid splits end the walk. No time limit. Undo reverses a split, but keeps attempt counts.
