Goal: rotate the crystal mirrors so a pulse travels from the glowing source to the matching receiver. Left/Right chooses a mirror, Space rotates it, Enter sends the pulse, P pauses, R restarts, T changes the chamber palette, and F toggles fullscreen. On touch, tap a mirror to rotate it.
