Teapot Pour

A LITTLE PATIENCE IN EVERY CUP

Hold Space or the Pour button. Release early: tea already falling takes 0.4 seconds to land. Stop inside the gold target band, then wait for the cup to settle. Fifteen cups, five services, three spills. Enter starts the next service. P pauses, R restarts, T changes colors, F toggles fullscreen; Escape exits. Tap and hold the canvas on a phone. More games
