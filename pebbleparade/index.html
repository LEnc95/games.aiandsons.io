Make four skips on each pond. Move the golden landing ring with ←/→, then press Space when the sweeping pebble passes through it. Each pond gets faster; three splashes end the run. Tap the water to move the ring and throw. P pauses, R restarts, T changes colors, F toggles fullscreen.
