Torch Bridge

ONE LANTERN. FOUR TRAVELERS.

Get all four travelers across before the lantern burns out.
Select one or two travelers on the lantern's bank, then cross.
A crossing costs the slower traveler's minutes. The lantern must return with someone.
Keys 1–4 toggle travelers · Space crosses · Enter starts/continues
P pauses · R restarts · F fullscreen · Escape exits.
