FOUR WITNESSES. ONE HIDDEN ACORN.

Acorn Alibis

One of four woodland friends hid the golden acorn. Each witness makes one statement. The case tells you exactly how many statements are true. Try each possible hider in your head: only one fits. Solve five cases before three wrong accusations.
