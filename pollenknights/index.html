TWO FLOCKS. ONE SMALL GARDEN.

Pollen Knights

Exchange the two flocks in 30 hops. Move all three honey knights to the bottom H nests and all three violet knights to the top V nests. Every move is a chess-knight hop: two squares, then one sideways. Either flock can move; occupied squares block landings.

Tap a knight, then a ringed empty square. Both flocks can move at any time.
Arrows move the square cursor · Enter / Space select or hop · U undo
P or Escape pause · R restart · F fullscreen · Escape exits fullscreen.
No captures or time limit. Undo restores pieces but keeps the hop count. Reach all six nests before the 30th hop ends.
