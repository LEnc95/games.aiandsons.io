Goal: catch four sunbeams in each of five garden clocks. Turn the dial with arrows or A/D and press Space, tap the dial, or use Catch when the gold hand overlaps the bright arc. Three missed shadows end the sprint. P pauses, R restarts, T changes stonework, and F toggles fullscreen.
